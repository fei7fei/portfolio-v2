About
Michael Xie builds hardware and software that solve real problems. He is an Electrical Engineering student at the University of British Columbia, graduating in May 2027, and enjoys turning repetitive, frustrating problems into tools that just work.

He has interned at the UBC MEMS Lab, HYC Technology and Suzhou Yi Li Energy, on everything from cardiac-tissue test rigs to PCB diagnostics and repair. He also researches medical-imaging AI, studying how chest X-ray classifiers perform in practice, not just how they score on accuracy.
